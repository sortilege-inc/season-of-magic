Bracers of Archery

Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)

While wearing these bracers, you have proficiency with the longbow and shortbow, and you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls on ranged attacks made with such weapons.

Part of the reward for breaking the Thayan attack on the standing stones in Arcana Unleashed, alongside bone carvings worth 250 gold and a potion of lightning resistance.

Attuned to Princess Hobnip, whose longbow now reads 1d8+6.

Source · Item text supplied by the player; awarded for the fight at the standing stones in session one
