Season of Magic

A season of Forgotten Realms organized play, table by table.

A Dungeons & Dragons chronicle · Forgotten Realms · 2024 rules (5.5e)

Season of Magic is a run of Forgotten Realms organized play on the 2024 rules: shared evenings where several tables work the same ground at once, able to talk across the gap, trade across it and heal across it — and, afterwards, to compare how much of the enemy each of them got. This is the archive of one of those tables: the characters who keep coming back, the nights they came through, and what they carried out.
